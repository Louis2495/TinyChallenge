⭐ TinyChallenge Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

TinyChallenge does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your email address, phone number, or location.

Data Stored on Your Device

TinyChallenge stores your progress directly on your device. This may include:

  • Completed challenges
  • Favorites
  • Journal entries
  • Streaks
  • Coins
  • Avatar customizations
  • Reminder preferences
  • Music preferences

This information is not transmitted to us.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, TinyChallenge schedules local notifications on your device. These reminders are created entirely on your device and are not sent from a server.

Music

Background music included with TinyChallenge is bundled with the app and plays locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

TinyChallenge does not sell your information.

TinyChallenge currently does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or account services.

Your Data

Because your information is stored locally, deleting the app will permanently remove your stored data unless you have backed up your device.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the TinyChallenge Support page.
